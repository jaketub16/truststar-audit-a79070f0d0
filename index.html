What the data does support

At about $1,500 a week since mid-September — especially the Admiral’s Letter campaign — bookings got cheaper, not more expensive. What the data does not support

It does not yet prove that keeping $1,500/week is throwing off cash. The bottleneck looks like shows and collected revenue, not raw lead volume. Steve has about a 40-call backlog. Real pipeline math

Four layers, kept apart: cash already real, face-value if panels enroll, probability-weighted only where a close rate is sourced, and break-even on ad spend. Formulas are written out so anyone can redo them.

1. Realized revenue
