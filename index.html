The biggest lever right now is show rate

From April through mid-October you recorded 57 unique bookings and 13 shows — a 22.8% show rate — and 3 YES practices. Those 3 YES total $690,000 a year of potential TrustStar revenue if the panels fully enroll, or $230,000 on average. That is full-enrollment annual potential, not cash already collected and not profit. It depends on how many patients actually enroll, and on how long that takes.

The table below is a historical what-if on those same 57 bookings. It asks: if more of those already-booked meetings had shown, and if they had closed at the same 3-out-of-13 rate you actually recorded, what would the face-value opportunity have looked like? It is not a forecast of next quarter’s new bookings, and it does not add new deals on top of the 3 YES or the proposals still in discussion (Slaughter, Blanc, Pimentel, Gaber). The 3-of-13 close rate is a small sample, used here only as an explicit assumption — not a validated prediction. We are not saying every no-show can be converted, and we are not saying the ads themselves cannot still improve.
